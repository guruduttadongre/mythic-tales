Once upon a time, in a lively forest filled with the chatter of birds and the rustle of leaves, there lived a rabbit named Ria and a tortoise named Tanu. Ria was known far and wide for her incredible speed. She loved to race and always bragged about how fast she could run. Tanu, on the other hand, was slow and steady, always taking his time.

One sunny morning, as the animals gathered by the big oak tree, Ria boasted once again, "I'm the fastest in the forest! No one can beat me!" Hearing this, Tanu smiled gently [gentle] and said, "Ria, let's have a race. You might be surprised!"

The other animals gasped. How could a slow tortoise like Tanu ever think of racing Ria? But Tanu stood firm [determined], and Ria accepted the challenge with a confident grin.

The day of the big race arrived. The animals gathered along the path, chattering with excitement. The race would begin at the big oak tree and end at the sparkling blue river.

As the race started, Ria dashed off in a blur, racing ahead like the wind. Tanu, however, moved slowly, step by careful step, but with a heart full of determination.

Ria quickly reached the halfway point and looked back, seeing Tanu far behind. She chuckled, thinking, "I have plenty of time to rest," and lay down under a shady tree, soon falling into a deep, comfortable sleep.

Meanwhile, Tanu kept going, never stopping. His little legs moved steadily, and he hummed a cheerful tune to keep his spirits up.

The sun began to dip in the sky, and the animals watched in awe as Tanu inched closer to the finish line. Just then, Ria awoke with a start. Seeing Tanu almost at the river, panic filled her [nervous]. She jumped up and raced as fast as she could.

But it was too late. Tanu, with a final burst of energy [triumphant], crossed the finish line just as Ria arrived, panting and astonished.

The forest erupted in cheers for Tanu. Ria, though disappointed, approached Tanu and said, "Congratulations, Tanu. I was too proud, and you taught me a valuable lesson."

Tanu smiled warmly [gentle]. "Thank you, Ria. Slow and steady can win the race, especially when you never give up."

And from that day on, Ria and Tanu became great friends, racing together for fun, and Ria learned to respect everyone in the forest for their unique strengths.

**Reflection:**

Tanu showed us that even if we're not the fastest or the strongest, perseverance and determination can help us achieve our goals. Just like Tanu, if you keep going and never give up, you can do amazing things!
