title: Crocodile, monkey and the jamun fruit
source: Indian folklore and kids stories
age_range: 6-10 years

Once upon a time, by a sparkling river, there lived a clever monkey named Miko. Miko loved to swing through the trees, munching on juicy jamun fruits all day. His best friend was a crocodile named Coco, who swam in the river nearby.

One sunny day, Coco came to Miko with a special request. [gentle] "Dear Miko, could you give me some of your delicious jamun fruits? My wife would love to try them."

Miko happily shared his jamun fruits, and Coco took them home. But Coco's wife had a different idea. "These fruits are so tasty! I bet the monkey's heart would be even tastier since he eats them every day!"

The next day, Coco felt troubled. He didn't want to harm his friend, but he also wanted to make his wife happy. [nervous] So, he thought of a plan. "Miko," Coco called, "would you like to visit my home across the river?"

Miko loved adventures and agreed. Halfway across the river, Coco told Miko about his wife's wish. [quick-thinking] Quick-thinking Miko chuckled and said, "Oh, silly me! I left my heart back in the tree with the jamun fruits. We must go back!"

Coco believed Miko and turned back. As soon as they reached the riverbank, Miko leaped to safety. [triumphant] "Always keep a light heart, Coco!" Miko said with a smile.

And from that day on, Coco realized the true sweetness was in their friendship.

---

**Reflection:** Miko teaches us to think quickly and stay calm even when things are tough. Remember, being clever and kind can help solve any problem!
