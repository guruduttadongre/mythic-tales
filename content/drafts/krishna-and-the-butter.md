In the charming village of Vrindavan, there lived a playful little boy named Krishna. With his sparkling eyes and a cheeky smile, Krishna was loved by everyone, especially his mother, Yashoda. But Krishna had a favorite thing in the whole wide world—fresh, creamy butter!

One sunny morning, while Yashoda was busy, Krishna tiptoed quietly into the kitchen. He spotted a pot of butter sitting high on a shelf. His eyes twinkled with excitement. How could he reach it? Krishna called his little friends and together they formed a tower, with Krishna at the top.

Just as he grabbed the pot, Yashoda appeared! "Krishna!" she exclaimed, trying to look stern but failing to hide her smile. Krishna giggled, offering her the butter with his sticky hands. "Would you like some, Ma?" he asked with innocence in his eyes.

Yashoda couldn't help but laugh. She hugged Krishna, knowing his playful mischief was filled with love. And so, the village knew Krishna’s antics were never far from a heartwarming hug.

---

**Reflection:** 

Like Krishna, sometimes we can use our creativity to solve problems. But it’s important to remember that sharing moments with loved ones can bring the most joy.
