**The Tale of Shiva’s Blue Throat**

Once upon a time, in a land of gods and magic, the world was in great trouble. A dangerous poison, called Halahala, had risen from the ocean, threatening to destroy everything with its dark fumes. Both the gods, who lived in the sky, and the demons, who lived under the earth, were worried. They gathered together on the shore of the great ocean, hoping to find a way to save their beloved world.

Among the gods was Shiva, the kind and wise god known for his meditation and deep love for all living beings. He had long, matted hair, a serene face, and was often seen wearing a garland of snakes around his neck. Shiva listened carefully as the gods and demons argued about what to do with the poison. They knew whoever tried to stop it would be in great danger.

As the voices grew louder and the fear spread, Shiva stood up. “I will drink the poison,” he said calmly, his eyes shining with determination. “I cannot let this poison harm the world.”

The gods and demons gasped. This was a huge sacrifice, even for a strong god like Shiva. But Shiva was not afraid. He walked to the edge of the ocean, cupped his hands, and scooped up the swirling, dark poison.

With everyone watching in awe, Shiva drank the poison. As soon as it touched his lips, his neck turned a deep, vibrant blue. The poison was powerful, but Shiva’s strength and love for the world were greater. He held the poison in his throat, preventing it from reaching his heart. From that day on, he was known as Neelkantha, the Blue-Throated One.

Shiva’s brave act saved the world. The skies cleared, and the air became fresh again. Flowers bloomed, and the birds sang happily. The gods and demons cheered for Shiva, grateful for his sacrifice and courage.

“Thank you, Shiva,” they said, bowing with respect. “You have saved us all with your selflessness.”

Shiva smiled, feeling the warmth of their gratitude. He didn’t seek praise or rewards. He was happy knowing that the world would continue to thrive, with children playing, rivers flowing, and life blossoming everywhere.

The gods promised to remember Shiva’s bravery and to always strive to protect and care for the world in their own ways. The demons, too, promised to work towards a better, kinder world, inspired by Shiva’s selfless act.

As the sun set, painting the sky with colors of gold and pink, Shiva returned to his home in the mountains, where he sat in peaceful meditation. His blue throat became a symbol of his sacrifice, reminding everyone of the power of love and courage.

---

**Reflection**

Shiva’s story teaches us that true bravery comes from caring for others, even when it’s difficult. Just like Shiva, you can be a hero by standing up for what is right and helping those in need. Remember, sometimes the greatest strength is the love and kindness you show to the world.
